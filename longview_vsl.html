What LongView actually does

Comprehensive financial planning, built for physicians and dentists.

We’re a fiduciary firm — just one team that coordinates every moving piece of your financial life so the decisions stop competing with each other.

Student loans & cash flow
PSLF, SAVE/IDR coordination, refinance vs. forgiveness math — modeled against your actual income and household, not generic rules of thumb.
Tax planning that compounds
Pre-RMD window strategy, backdoor / mega-backdoor Roth, charitable bunching, and retirement account sequencing — not just filing in April.
Retirement & investment strategy
Coordinating 8–12 scattered accounts into one coherent structure, with a withdrawal plan that protects against tax and sequence-of-returns risk.

Likely a fit if…

  • You’re a physician or dentist juggling 5+ financial decisions at once
  • You want someone who coordinates loans, taxes, investments, and insurance together
  • You’d rather work with a fiduciary than someone whose advice changes based on what you buy
  • You’re comfortable making decisions once you have the math in front of you

Probably not a fit if…

  • You’re looking for a single product to buy or a hot stock pick
  • You only want help filing your taxes once a year
  • You want someone to make every decision for you with no input
  • You’re not ready to look honestly at where things are today
