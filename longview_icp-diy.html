What We Cover

Every corner of your financial picture

One conversation. We look at everything — not to take it over, but to tell you what's working, what isn't, and what you might be missing.

Student Loan Strategy

PSLF eligibility, repayment plan assumptions, IDR strategy, refinancing decision — the math is wrong more often than physicians expect.

Tax Efficiency

Backdoor Roth, solo 401(k), practice entity structure, deferred comp — the filing isn't the planning. We look at what you're not capturing.

Investment Allocation

Asset allocation, fee drag, tax-location strategy, account sequencing. Most DIY physicians have the right accounts — just not the right structure across them.

Insurance & Risk

Disability, life, umbrella, malpractice — most attending physicians are under-insured on both life and disability.

Retirement Timeline

Are the numbers actually right? Your savings rate, projected spending, withdrawal strategy — and whether the exit number in your head is real.

Estate & Beneficiaries

Beneficiary designations, titling, basic estate structure — often untouched for years after major life changes. Wrong in ways that are expensive to discover late.
