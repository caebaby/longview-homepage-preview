THE PROBLEM

The math doesn't add up for most physicians.

Illustrative examples of common physician situations — not client testimonials

High income, complex tax situation, student loans, a practice, a family — and somehow the net worth doesn't reflect any of it. You're not alone, and it's not your fault.

Student Loans
My loan repayment plan just got pulled out from under me.

PSLF. SAVE. IDR. The rules keep changing and the stakes keep rising. If you qualified under one program and now you don't, you're not behind — you've been failed by a system that keeps moving the target. We track this so you don't have to.

Net Worth Gap
I make good money. I should have more to show for it.

High income doesn't automatically become wealth. Attendings leaving residency face a transition problem: the same habits that got you through training are actively working against building net worth. Filing is not planning.

Retirement Readiness
My spouse asked 'when can you retire?' and I didn't have an answer.

After 20+ years of sacrifice, you should be able to answer that question. Most physicians can't — not because they haven't earned it, but because nobody's ever mapped their whole financial picture and told them the number. We do that.

Advisor Risk
I've been sold products by people who said they specialized in doctors.

If you've been recommended financial products and weren't sure whether they fit your situation, you're not alone. When we act in an advisory capacity, we operate under a fiduciary standard — required to act in your best interest — and we're glad to give a second opinion on anything you already own. Our advisory fee is structured to be consistent and is not based on what you buy.

AREAS WE COVER

Everything your financial life needs. Nothing it doesn't.

We don't manage just your portfolio. We manage the whole picture — because for physicians and dentists, the whole picture can be complicated.

Student Loan Strategy

PSLF, IDR, SAVE, refinancing — we model the scenarios that matter and build a repayment strategy that survives rule changes. For physicians carrying significant student debt, this single decision is often one of the most consequential financial choices we'll work on together.

Tax Planning (Not Just Filing)

Filing is not planning. We work year-round to reduce your tax burden — income, practice, investments, estate — not just document it in April.

Retirement & Financial Independence

Not "retire and play golf." Work-optional — so you can practice medicine on your own terms, not because you have to. We'll give you a number and a timeline.

Investment Management

Evidence-based portfolios built around your plan, not around any product outcome. We focus on keeping investment costs transparent and aligned with your goals.

Insurance & Risk Analysis

Disability. Malpractice. Life. We review what you have, flag what you may be missing, and help you structure disability and life coverage the right way for your situation. As your advisor, our recommendation is based on your needs, not on any product outcome.

Practice Transitions & Business Planning

Partnership buy-in. Ownership. Practice transitions. Benefit and retirement plan decisions. These aren't just financial events — they have the potential to restructure everything downstream.
