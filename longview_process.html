Planning is personal.
Ours treats it that way.

Financial planning revolves around your personal goals and objectives such as retiring when and how you choose, educating your children or grandchildren, and protecting your family’s lifestyle. A properly constructed financial plan can improve your life by helping to reduce your financial stress and providing a feeling of confidence.

To do so it must address the many areas of your financial world and be kept current on a regular basis to adapt the inevitable changes in your life journey.

Every LongView client receives a customized strategy built around their values — created collaboratively with our team of CFP®, CPA, and MBA professionals, coordinating with your estate planning attorney, accountant, and other advisors when needed.

The Four Phases

Our planning process

A continuous cycle — each phase builds on the last and feeds the next. This is not a one-time engagement. It’s an ongoing relationship calibrated to wherever you are in your career.

Phase 01
Discover
Explore · Identify
Gather · Affirm

We learn everything about you — income, debts, benefits, goals, and fears. No judgment, no product pitch, no agenda. Just a complete picture of where you are today and where you want to go.

Phase 02
Plan
Organize · Design
Test · Recommend

We build your customized financial plan — every recommendation tied directly to your goals and values, stress-tested against real scenarios. A clear framework for making financial decisions with confidence, not guesswork.

Phase 03
Implement*
Construct · Analyze
Execute · Protect

Your plan becomes real. We coordinate with your accountant, estate planning attorney, and other advisors to execute — taking complexity off your plate so you can focus on medicine.

*Financial plan recommendations can be implemented with the advisor of your choosing. Implementation of specific products or services may result in commissions or fees outside of the financial plan fee. Periodic reviews may require a new planning agreement and result in additional fees.

Phase 04
Guide
Monitor · Measure
Review · Align

Life changes. Your plan adapts. We meet regularly to keep your financial life aligned as your career, family, and goals evolve. A plan that isn’t reviewed is just a document — this is where the ongoing relationship earns its keep.
