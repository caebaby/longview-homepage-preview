Not a Physician?

We built LongView for clinicians.
Here’s what that means for you.

Our entire practice is designed around the financial lives of physicians and dentists — and we’ve kept it that way on purpose. If you’re not a clinician, you can still talk to us: we’ll be honest about whether we’re the right fit, and point you in a good direction either way.

SEC Registered Representatives Built for Clinicians

Why we focus on physicians and dentists

Medicine and dentistry come with a financial life unlike any other: years of training on a resident’s income, a sudden jump to a high salary, six-figure student debt, contract and practice decisions, and a compressed timeline to build wealth. We’ve built every part of our planning around those realities — and we’ve deliberately chosen not to be generalists.

That focus is exactly what makes our advice valuable to the clients we serve. It also means we’re upfront when someone’s situation falls outside it — including pointing you toward someone who would serve you better.

Still Worth a Conversation

How we can still point you in the right direction

You’re family of a client

When we plan for a physician or dentist, we plan for the whole household. If you’re the spouse or partner of someone we work with, your financial life is already part of the picture — let’s talk.

You were referred to us

If a client or colleague sent you our way, reach out. Even when we’re not the right long-term fit, we’re glad to talk through your situation and help you find an advisor who is.

Your situation genuinely fits

Once in a while, someone outside medicine has needs that line up closely with how we work. Book a 30-minute Icebreaker Call and we’ll be honest, on the call, about whether we can help — or who would serve you better.

Sound Like You?

The Icebreaker Call is where we find out.

30 minutes. No pitch, no agenda. We'll ask about your situation, you'll ask about us, and we'll both decide whether it makes sense to keep going. No pressure — we mean that.
