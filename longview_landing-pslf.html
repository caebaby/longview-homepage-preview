Is This For You?

Qualify yourself first

✅ This is for you if…
› You’re an attending physician with federal student loans still outstanding
› You enrolled in SAVE or another IDR plan and haven’t reviewed it since SAVE was blocked in court
› You’re at a nonprofit or government employer and counting on PSLF to forgive your balance
› You’re not sure if your payment history is counting correctly toward forgiveness
› You want a second opinion before making an irreversible decision, like refinancing out of federal loans
❌ This is NOT for you if…
› You’ve already paid off your student loans in full
› You have only private student loans (private loans are not eligible for PSLF or IDR plans)
