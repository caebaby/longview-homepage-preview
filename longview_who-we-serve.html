Early Career Physicians & Dentists

You didn't go to medical school to become a financial expert.

You dedicated a decade to becoming exceptional at medicine. Meanwhile, the financial system moved on without you — and in your absence, financial decisions got made without the kind of objective advice a fiduciary provides.

Your income finally looks right. But your financial life hasn't caught up. That's not a character flaw. That's what transitioning from training looks like. We've guided hundreds of physicians and dentists through your exact moment — and we know the sequence that gets you from here to ahead.

We work with early career clinicians across all specialties — from primary care to surgery, from residents still in training to attendings 5 years into their first real job.

Mid Career Physicians & Dentists

The chaos cleared. But the clarity never came.

You're past the loan crisis. You're a decade into your career, earning well, and living well. But your financial picture has quietly gotten complicated — multiple jobs, scattered accounts, a tax bill that surprises you every April, and zero time to address any of it.

This is the stage where the gap between income and actual wealth starts to feel permanent. Not because you've done anything wrong, but because nobody ever told you the plan was supposed to change as your career matured.

We work with mid-career physicians and dentists who are high-earners with underperforming financial plans — and help them catch up faster than they thought possible.

Dentists

We serve dentists with the same depth we serve physicians — not as an afterthought.

Most "physician-focused" firms tag dentists on as a footnote. We don't. The financial challenges of dental practice are specific enough that generic advice — even physician-focused advice — misses the mark.

Student debt loads rival medical school. Practice ownership is the norm rather than the exception. PPO compression and DSO consolidation are reshaping the economics of dentistry in real time. We know this world.

Student Debt
Average dental school debt is substantial. The loan strategy for dentists has different levers than medicine — and many advisors don't know the difference.
Practice Ownership
Most dentists own or plan to own their practice. Business planning, buy-in structure, and exit strategy are integrated into your financial plan — not treated as a separate problem.
DSO & PE Pressure
DSO acquisition offers are landing on dentist desks at an accelerating rate. Understanding your financial position before you evaluate an offer changes the decision entirely.
PPO Compression
Reimbursement rates are shrinking. We model your practice cash flow as part of your personal financial plan so you can see exactly how the business decision affects the family balance sheet.

We're probably not the right fit for everyone.

We'd rather be honest about this upfront than waste your time or ours. Here's who we're genuinely not a great match for — and why we're saying so out loud.

Anyone who isn't a physician or dentist Our entire model is built around clinical careers. If you're not a clinician, there are excellent advisors who are a better fit for your situation.
DIY investors who want a second opinion but not a planner We're a comprehensive planning firm, not a portfolio reviewer. If you want someone to manage investments and not be heard from again, that's not how we work.
People looking for the lowest possible fee We charge fairly for comprehensive planning. If the primary decision variable is cost, the math won't work — and you'd resent what you're paying before we've done anything useful.
Anyone who wants investment products Operating under a fiduciary standard when acting in an advisory capacity, we're required to act in your interest. Ask us how we're compensated, and we'll tell you. If you have questions about whether a product you've been recommended is right for you, that's exactly what an icebreaker call is for.
