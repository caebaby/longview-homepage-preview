What We See in Physicians at Every Stage

You’re not behind because you’re bad with money.
You’re behind because nobody built you a real plan.

Here’s what the assessment surfaces in many physicians we talk to — regardless of income, specialty, or years in practice.

The Debt Problem
“I’ve been paying my loans for years and I’m not sure I’m doing it right.”
PSLF, IDR, refinancing — the right answer depends on your employer, your income trajectory, and your family situation. Many physicians are on the wrong plan. The difference can be substantial.
The Tax Problem
“My accountant files my taxes. I assumed that was the same as planning.”
Filing is not planning. High-income physicians often overpay in taxes because filing looks backward. Planning looks forward. Backdoor Roth, defined benefit plans, S-corp elections — these decisions happen before December, not April.
The Clarity Problem
“I make good money. I should feel more confident about where I stand.”
Accounts scattered across institutions. Insurance you’re not sure you need. No clear retirement number. No one who can look at all of it together and tell you the truth. That’s what the Icebreaker Call is for.
