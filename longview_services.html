What We Cover

The most common areas where physicians lose money.

We mapped the financial picture of a physician or dentist from training to retirement — and built a process that addresses the areas where the wrong decision is expensive.

Student Loan Strategy

PSLF, IDR, SAVE, refinancing — we model the scenarios that matter and build a repayment strategy that survives rule changes. For physicians carrying significant student debt, this single decision is often one of the most consequential financial choices we'll work on together. Especially urgent: the PSLF/SAVE landscape changed. If your strategy is 2+ years old, it needs a review.

Tax Planning (Not Just Filing)

Filing is not planning. We work year-round to reduce your tax burden — income, practice, investments, estate — not just document it in April. Many physicians with attending-level income may be over-paying taxes from strategies they don't know to use.

Retirement & Financial Independence

Not "retire and play golf." Work-optional — so you can practice medicine on your own terms, not because you have to. We model your number, your timeline, and the exact savings rate needed to get there.

Investment Management

Evidence-based and built around your plan and your outcome — not around any particular product, with a focus on keeping investment costs transparent. Asset allocation, tax-location strategy, and coordination across every account you own.

Insurance & Risk Analysis

Disability. Malpractice. Life. Umbrella. We review what you have, flag what you're missing, and help you structure disability and life coverage the right way for your situation. As your advisor, our recommendation is based on your situation, not on any product outcome. (Most attending physicians are under-insured on both life and disability.)

Practice Transitions & Business Planning

Partnership buy-ins, practice transitions, benefit planning, and retirement plan decisions can change your personal financial picture overnight. We help you plan around the personal outcome: how to manage the tax impact, coordinate the financial pieces, and fit the new reality into your long-term plan.

Why "comprehensive" isn't a marketing word for us

Most advisors specialize in one or two areas — usually the ones that generate the most revenue. You end up with a great investment portfolio, no tax strategy, and a student loan plan that made sense in 2019.

We mapped every area where physicians lose money — and built a practice that addresses all of them. Not because it makes for a good pitch. Because leaving one area unaddressed usually costs more than everything else combined.
